Level 2 · Control — ASU1 DCS

DCS · operator console

ASU1 Operator Console

Process overview, controller faceplates (MAN/AUTO/CAS), alarms, interlocks, the PPU switching sequence and equipment start/stop. 🎓 Training (top right) walks students through the process step by step, with what each step is doing right now.

 DCS · built-in historian

Historian trends

Every PV, SP and OP, exception-reported into the DCS's own historian, plus the alarm & event journal. Up to 8 pens, 15 min – 7 days.
DCS · OPC UA server (read-only)

OPC UA endpoint

opc.tcp://localhost:4840/hcw/asu1/

Anonymous, no security. Namespace urn:haltenberg:hcw:asu1:dcs, browse Objects/HCW/Haltenberg/ASU1. NodeIds look like ns=2;s=HCW.ASU1.FIC-101.PV. PV/SP/OP are AnalogItems with units and range, and support HistoryRead from the DCS historian.

Level 3–4 · Integration — OPC UA clients

Integration · Node-RED ↗

Node-RED

Flow editor. The pre-loaded flow OPC UA → 15-min TWA → CSV subscribes to six ASU1 tags on the DCS's OPC UA server and computes a time-weighted average per 15-minute window.
Integration · CSV export

15-minute averages

exports/asu1_twa_15min.csv in the project folder — one row per window: air load, GOX send-out, header pressure, purity, CO₂, specific power, plus coverage % (how much of the window had good-quality data). The first row appears at the next quarter-hour.

Level 0–1 · Process & field I/O

Training simulator · not part of the plant

Instructor Station

Starts fault and disturbance scenarios (MAC trip, mole-sieve breakthrough, demand steps, fouling, …) and shows the simulator's hidden ground truth. Nothing here is visible to the DCS or over OPC UA.
Field level · Modbus TCP

Raw field I/O rack

localhost:5020 unit 1 — float32 register pairs, no units, no hierarchy, no quality. The register map is listed on the instructor station. It's the raw layer the DCS turns into the modelled OPC UA view.
